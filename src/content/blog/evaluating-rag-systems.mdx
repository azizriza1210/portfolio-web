## Beyond "Does it work?"

Evaluating RAG systems requires measuring both retrieval and generation quality separately. A correct answer with poor sources is still a broken system.

## Retrieval Metrics

- **Recall@k** — What fraction of relevant documents appear in the top k results?
- **MRR** — Mean Reciprocal Rank — how early does the first relevant doc appear?
- **NDCG** — Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain — rewards relevant docs ranked higher.

## Generation Metrics

- **Faithfulness** — Is the answer grounded in the retrieved context?
- **Answer Relevance** — Does the answer actually address the question?
- **Context Relevance** — Are the retrieved documents relevant to the query?

## Tools

Use RAGAS, TruLens, or DeepEval for systematic evaluation. Don't rely on vibe checks.
