## Why Chunking Matters

The way you split documents into chunks directly impacts retrieval quality. Too small, and you lose context. Too large, and you dilute the embedding signal.

## Common Strategies

### Fixed-size Chunking
Split by character count. Simple but often breaks mid-sentence.

### Semantic Chunking
Split at natural boundaries — paragraphs, sections, sentences.

### Recursive Chunking
Split recursively by separators: `\n\n` → `\n` → ` ` → `""`.

### Overlap
Add overlapping text between chunks to preserve context across boundaries.

## Recommendation

Start with recursive chunking with 10-20% overlap. Measure retrieval quality, then tune.
